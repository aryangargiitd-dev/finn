# FINN demo — approximately four minutes

## 0:00–0:40 — Start with the investor's question

“FINN turns market noise into a short, inspectable research workflow. What changed, why should I care, and what do the original documents support?”
Open Pulse at mobile width. Show the mock 18-company watchlist and the EOD label. Point out the session date and cached/fetched labels. Do not call these live quotes. If today's feed is empty, explain the rolling RSS limitation and choose a genuine wider window; never imply a historical event happened today.

## 0:40–1:20 — Earn attention

Show a material filing and expand “Why it matters.” Explain category, materiality, recency and routine-notice penalties. Open its original filing. Reveal routine disclosures. Switch company/priority/time filters. Explain that a filing's attention score is not a share-price prediction.

## 1:20–2:00 — Define unusual

Open a company to inspect price history, daily volume and the prior-20-session baseline. Open Settings, change a threshold, save, and show Alerts changing. Mention persistence on this browser. Monitoring occurs on page load/refresh; it is not background push. Restore defaults if desired.

## 2:00–2:50 — Research the evidence

Open Research → INFY → Financials and Sources. Point out FY2025, consolidated IFRS, USD million, and page-level links. Show annual revenue trajectory and explain reported USD growth differs from constant-currency growth. Ask about revenue guidance; show retrieved evidence and its citations. No-key mode is extractive, not a claimed LLM answer.

For generalisation, search COFORGE, a company outside the prepared notebooks. Let it retrieve a BSE transcript. The live workflow was verified during the build, but if the source is temporarily unavailable, show the honest error and return to the verified notebook. Newly parsed passages do not automatically become unverified financial cards.

## 2:50–3:40 — The differentiator

Open INFY → Reality Check. “In January 2025, management guided FY2025 constant-currency growth to 4.5–5%. In April it reported 4.2%. On a matched basis, that's 0.3 percentage points below the lower bound.”
Show the exact quotation, speaker, statement date and both document links. Explain why it compares a specific guidance vintage and does not attribute causes or imply future performance. For another company, an evidence gap is preferable to a forced verdict.

## 3:40–4:10 — Architecture and judgment

“One edge deployment, server-side source adapters, deterministic calculations, bounded document retrieval, optional model synthesis and transparent cache states. I kept the difficult accounting comparisons honest and shipped the useful workflow first.”
Close with next steps: durable scheduled ingestion, broader document discovery, reviewed financial extraction and shared portfolios. Show the repository, test report and genuine build history.
